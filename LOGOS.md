# Logos

SVG logos with all text converted to outlines, so they render without fonts. Colors are fixed in the files (an `<img>` cannot recolor them): civic red #B31942 (light red #E0566E on black), peacock blue #016795, black #101010, linen #F1EFE7.

- `rta-logo-horizontal-tagline.svg` — mark + wordmark + "government, made clear."; transparent; the default lockup.
- `rta-logo-horizontal.svg` — mark + wordmark; transparent.
- `rta-logo-horizontal-reversed.svg` — on black, with tagline.
- `rta-logo-primary.svg` — stacked wordmark; transparent.
- `rta-logo-tagline.svg` — stacked wordmark with tagline; transparent.
- `rta-logo-primary-linen.svg` — stacked wordmark on a linen field.
- `rta-logo-reversed.svg` / `rta-logo-tagline-reversed.svg` — stacked, on black.
- `rta-mark.svg` — civic-cap mark alone; transparent.
- `rta-mark-reversed.svg` — civic-cap mark on a peacock square; avatars and favicons.
- `rta-monogram-peacock.svg` / `rta-monogram-linen.svg` — "rta" monograms.
